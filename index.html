Roblox Friend Analyzer

Ranks a user's public friends by account age, followers and estimated equipped-avatar value. No login, cookies or tokens are ever requested.

Default: DrRobuxic (2968372123). User IDs, not usernames.

Avatar value is the estimated current Robux price of the items a friend has equipped right now. It is not inventory wealth and says nothing about how many Robux someone owns. Items without a public price are shown as Unavailable and left out of the total.
